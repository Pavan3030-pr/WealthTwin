# 4-minute finale demo

1. **Hook (20s)** Landing page: "People find out about a cash crunch when it is too late."
2. **Overview (40s)** Dashboard for Aarav: health score and its five factors.
3. **Persona switch (30s)** Switch to Meera (freelancer). Forecast page shows the squeeze date and downside band.
4. **Simulator (60s)** Preset "Medical bill ₹1.5L", then "Salary +15%". Show score, balance and goal odds change live.
5. **Copilot (45s)** Ask "Can I afford a ₹40,000 phone?" and show the basis chips.
6. **Learn (20s)** Open a lesson; point to "In your numbers".
7. **Close (25s)** Business model, Account Aggregator roadmap, ask.

Backup: if offline, everything runs locally; the Copilot falls back to the built-in engine.
